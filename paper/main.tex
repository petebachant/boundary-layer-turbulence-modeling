\section{Introduction}\label{sec:introduction}

% TODO (human): skeleton; facts only.

A RANS closure is used with one set of coefficients. Data-driven closures
are, in the published record, mostly excellent on the flow they were built
from and untested or worse than their baseline elsewhere
\citep{Duraisamy2019, Brunton2020, SandbergZhao2022, Nishi2024,
RumseyColemanWang2022}; coefficient posteriors are known to move between
boundary-layer flows \citep{Edeling2014}, and damping functions have been
fitted to DNS budgets since \citet{RodiMansour1993}.

This paper reports two things. The first is a transition closure whose
activation source is a clipped signal (\S\ref{sec:closure}): a
$k$--$\omega$--$\gamma$ model with \result[NFittedCoeffs]{} coefficients
fitted once on one DNS, together with the structural variants, the ablation,
the search-noise measurement and a momentum-term extension that were tested
alongside it. The second is the suite used to test it (\S\ref{sec:cases},
\S\ref{sec:gym}): \finding[benchmark_n_cases] fast-tier and
\finding[tier2_n_cases] OpenFOAM cases from DNS and LES in seven geometries,
each with declared targets, scored by a rule that never averages the cases a
closure was fitted on with the cases it was not. Every closure of
\S\ref{sec:models} was run on every case of its tier with its coefficients
unchanged; the full matrices are Tables~\ref{tab:fast-matrix} and
\ref{tab:openfoam-matrix}. The closure that scores best on its calibration
case transfers worst in the fast tier; Launder--Sharma with published
coefficients transfers best there and diverges on two of the four hills in
OpenFOAM; the published transition models lead the OpenFOAM tier; and no
closure tested holds across the suite with one coefficient set.

\section{Reference cases}\label{sec:cases}

Each case is a flow with reference data, a way to run a closure on it, and a
declared target error per scored quantity. The normalized score of
\S\ref{sec:scoring} is the mean ratio of error to target, so $1$ means the
closure matches the data to within every declared target. Every case except
the transitional plate is out of sample for every closure in this paper.
Table~\ref{tab:cases} lists the suite; all reference data are fetched by the
pipeline and their sources recorded in the project metadata.

\emph{Transitional flat plate.} The JHTDB \texttt{transition\_bl} DNS
\citep{JHTDBDescription, Zaki2013}: bypass transition under free-stream
turbulence, $x \in [\result[XInlet], \result[XOutlet]]$,
$y \in [0, \result[YMax]]$, $\nu = 1.25\times10^{-3}$, $Tu = \result[TuInlet]\,\%$
and $\delta_{99} = \result[DeltaInlet]$ at the inlet station. Skin friction
reaches its minimum at $x \approx 205$ and its peak near $x \approx 450$. The
only case any closure here was calibrated on.

\emph{Zero-pressure-gradient boundary layer.} Six stations of the DNS of
\citet{Sillero2013} at $Re_\theta = 4000$--$6500$. The streamwise spacing is
recovered from $d\theta/dx = c_f/2$; the first station seeds the transported
scalars and is excluded from scoring.

\emph{Channel.} \citet{LeeMoser2015} at $Re_\tau = 180$, $1000$ and $5200$.
With $\delta = u_\tau = 1$ the total stress is linear and $U$ follows from
one integration once the closure supplies $\nu_t$; the closure equations are
relaxed to a fixed point, and a closure that reaches none scores $\infty$.

\emph{NACA 4412 suction side.} The LES of \citet{Vinuesa2018} at
$Re_c = 4\times10^5$ and $10^6$: adverse pressure gradient with the Clauser
parameter reaching $\beta = 112$ and $c_f$ falling to $1.8\times10^{-4}$
without separating. The only non-DNS reference; the resolved stresses omit
the sub-grid share.

\emph{Temporal mixing layer.} The DNS of \citet{Lusher2026} from the JAXA
DNS database \citep{JAXADNSDatabase}: a tanh shear layer at
$\Delta U L_x/\nu = 250{,}000$ that thickens as a plane mixing layer and
then rolls up \citep{Sansica2026}. Scored over $\hat t \in [0.14, 0.40]$,
before roll-up, with every closure seeded from the reference initial
condition. The only wall-free case.

\emph{Periodic hills.} Four cases of the parameterized family of
\citet{Xiao2020}, hill slopes $\alpha = 0.5$ ($Re = 4071$) and $1.5$
($Re = 13929$), each supplied by the Closure Challenge \citep{McConkey2026}
as two cases with different meshes (15\,600 cells each), DNS fields and
1000 evaluation points. The flow separates on the lee slope.

\emph{Square and rectangular ducts.} Four DNS of \citet{Vinuesa2014}, aspect
ratios 1, 3 and 14 at $Re_\tau = 180$ and $360$, from the same challenge:
streamwise-periodic, with a secondary flow driven by the normal-stress
anisotropy. A linear eddy-viscosity model sets that anisotropy to zero and
therefore scores exactly $1.0$ on the secondary-flow metric whatever its
coefficients.

\section{Closures tested and simulation setup}\label{sec:models}\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Closures}

Table~\ref{tab:closures} lists every closure run, the tier(s) it exists in,
and the cases its coefficients were fitted on. Published models were run
with their published coefficients: Launder--Sharma $k$--$\epsilon$
\citep{LaunderSharma1974}, $k$--$\omega$ SST \citep{Menter2006}, the
Langtry--Menter $\gamma$--$Re_{\theta t}$ model \citep{LangtryMenter2009}
and the Walters--Cokljat $k_L$--$k_T$--$\omega$ model \citep{Walters2008}. A
laminar (no-model) run is the lower bound. The clipping closure of
\S\ref{sec:closure} exists in both tiers with the same
\result[NFittedCoeffs]{} coefficients; its four fast-tier structural variants
--- an algebraic mixing-length form, a transported-$k$ form, a two-reservoir
streak/active split and an entropy-carrying variant --- and the two
momentum-library closures of \S\ref{sec:momentum-library} were fitted on the
plate, or on the four cases named in the table, and run everywhere else
unchanged.

\subsection{Solvers}

The fast tier is a parabolic boundary-layer marching solver, which advances
the mean momentum and closure equations downstream from the reference inlet
profile in about a second per candidate, with two exact-formulation cases
(channel, temporal mixing layer) in which the one-dimensional equations are
integrated directly. Against the Blasius solution it reproduces the skin
friction to within \finding[screening_solver_cf_error_pct]\,\%. Its bias
against the elliptic solve is measured in \S\ref{sec:generality}.

The OpenFOAM tier is OpenFOAM v2412 \texttt{simpleFoam} (steady SIMPLE). On
the plate the domain is a single block matched to the DNS extent,
$x \in [\result[XInlet], \result[XOutlet]]$ and $y \in [0, \result[YMax]]$,
with 80 cells in $y$ graded to a first cell at $y^+ \lesssim 1$. On the
Closure Challenge cases the challenge's meshes are used as supplied, with
the DNS interpolated onto the same mesh and scored at the challenge's
evaluation points by nearest cell; one set of derived initial fields
($\epsilon$, $\gamma$, $k_L$, $k_T$, $Re_{\theta t}$) is built from the DNS
$k$ and $\omega$ by the same rule for every model; and one convergence
criterion per family (residuals of $10^{-6}$ on the streamwise velocity and
the transported scalars, capped at 20\,000 iterations) is applied to every
model. No setting is tuned per model.

\subsection{Inlet and initial conditions}

On the plate every model receives the DNS profile of $U$ and $k$ at the
inlet station. The inlet $\omega$ is fitted per model to the same measured
free-stream decay, because under
$k \propto (1 + \beta \omega_0 (x-x_{\rm in})/U)^{-\beta^*/\beta}$ a
single $\omega_0$ gives models with different $\beta$ different decay. The
$\gamma$--$Re_{\theta t}$ inlet $Re_{\theta t}$ is taken from that model's
own correlation at the inlet intensity, $\result[ReThetatInlet]$.

On cases whose reference state is already turbulent, every closure is seeded
from the reference data by one rule --- $k$ from the measured stresses,
$\omega$ from the measured eddy viscosity $-\langle u'v'\rangle/(dU/dy)$,
$\gamma = 1$ --- and the seeded station or transient is excluded from
scoring. Unseeded, the channel case measures whether a low-Reynolds-number
model self-starts from $k \approx 0$, a fixed point of every closure here:
the same closure scored $128$ unseeded and $3.3$ seeded
\citep[cf.][\S3.1]{Sansica2026}.

\section{The clipping closure}\label{sec:closure}

\subsection{Form and coefficients}

The closure transports $k$, $\omega$ and an activation fraction $\gamma$,
with $\nu_t = \gamma k/\omega$ and the $\gamma$ source rectified (clipped)
about a threshold in the vorticity Reynolds number $Re_v = y^2\Omega/\nu$.
Its \result[NFittedCoeffs]{} coefficients were fitted on the plate by random
search (\result[NRandom]{} samples) followed by \result[NRefineRounds]{}
rounds of Gaussian refinement about the incumbent (\result[NRefine]{}
samples each, with shrinking width), minimizing a target-normalized sum of
relative errors in skin friction, mean velocity, momentum thickness,
free-stream decay and turbulence energy.

\subsection{What the DNS says about the form}\label{sec:structure}

In the plate DNS the Reynolds-stress anisotropy and the mean strain are
misaligned by \finding[misalignment_deg_pretransition]$^\circ$ before
transition and by \finding[misalignment_deg_turbulent]$^\circ$ in
equilibrium turbulence, which bounds any scalar eddy-viscosity closure,
including this one. Through transition the $u$--$v$ correlation coefficient
grows \finding[coherence_ratio]$\times$ while the energy partition changes by
\finding[anisotropy_ratio]$\times$, and the information shared between the
velocity components grows from \finding[total_correlation_bits_laminar] to
\finding[total_correlation_bits_turbulent] bits. The component entropy of
the partition falls from above unity to a minimum of \finding[entropy_min]
at $x = \finding[entropy_min_x]$ before recovering, so a constitutive
relation in entropy alone is not single-valued; implemented as a closure
(the entropy variant of Table~\ref{tab:closures}) it does not hold the
laminar state. The stored fraction of the energy routed into turbulence
peaks at \finding[peak_turbulent_storage_pct]\,\% at
$x = \finding[peak_turbulent_storage_x]$ and then collapses to a few
percent; the turbulent share of entropy rejection rises from
\finding[turbulent_rejection_fraction_low_Re] to
\finding[turbulent_rejection_fraction_high_Re] across the turbulent region.

\subsection{Ablation}\label{sec:ablation}

Removing one ingredient at a time and re-scoring in the fast tier: without
the rectifier the skin-friction error rises from
\finding[ablation_reference_cf_pct]\,\% to
\finding[ablation_no_clip_cf_pct]\,\%, above $k$--$\epsilon$ at
\finding[baseline_k_epsilon_cf_pct]\,\%; without the lift-up term it is
\finding[ablation_no_liftup_cf_pct]\,\%. The streak viscous-decay term and
the gate on $\omega$ production change the score by nothing. Every variant
of $Re_v$ beats every other gate driver tested; the fitted threshold
$\Lambda_c = \finding[fitted_threshold_Lambda_c]$ lies near the classical
$440$, and imposing $440$ raises the error to
\finding[ablation_classical_threshold_cf_pct]\,\%. An evolutionary search
over the structure of the activation source does not reproduce from run to
run (its top eight structures used a rectifier in one run and none in the
next) and supports no structural claim.

\subsection{Beyond eddy viscosity: a momentum-term library}\label{sec:momentum-library}

Six streamwise force terms built from wall-normal derivatives of $U$, $k$
and $\nu_t = k/\omega$ --- the two halves of the eddy-viscosity divergence
freed to differ, $k'$, $\sqrt{k}\,U'$, $k'U'/\omega$ and
$(k/\omega)U'^2/\sqrt{k}$ --- were added to Launder--Sharma with
dimensionless coefficients, every term Galilean invariant and vanishing with
$k$, and the coefficients found by Bayesian optimization of the
a-posteriori benchmark score with a diverged solve treated as infeasible.
Fitted on the plate alone the library cuts Launder--Sharma's score there
from \finding[momentum_library_single_base_in_sample] to
\finding[momentum_library_single_best_objective] in
\finding[momentum_library_single_n_evaluations] evaluations and raises its
out-of-sample mean from
\finding[momentum_library_single_base_out_of_sample_mean] to
\finding[momentum_library_single_out_of_sample_mean]. Fitted jointly on
the plate, the ZPG layer, the $Re_\tau = 1000$ channel and the mixing
layer, the optimum found in \finding[momentum_library_multi_n_evaluations]
evaluations (\finding[momentum_library_multi_n_diverged] diverged) is the
base closure itself, every coefficient zero, at
\finding[momentum_library_multi_best_objective]. The experiment is bounded
by the fast tier, a small budget in six dimensions, and the absence of a
posterior.

\section{The benchmark}\label{sec:gym}

The harness scores every registered closure on every registered case a
posteriori, in a solver. A closure is a class implementing three methods
(initialize, eddy viscosity, advance) in the fast tier, or the name of an
OpenFOAM turbulence model; a case owns how to run a closure on its flow,
what the reference data are, and the target per scored quantity. Both are
registered, and third-party closures or cases are picked up from a named
module without editing the package.

\subsection{Scoring across cases}\label{sec:scoring}

A case $c$ declares, for each metric $m$ it scores, a target error
$\tau_{c,m}$; the normalized score of closure $\mathcal{M}$ on case $c$ is
\begin{equation}
  s_c(\mathcal{M}) = \frac{1}{|M_c|} \sum_{m \in M_c}
    \frac{e_{c,m}(\mathcal{M})}{\tau_{c,m}}.
  \label{eq:score}
\end{equation}
Each closure declares the set $\mathcal{C}(\mathcal{M})$ of cases its
coefficients were fitted on, and the leaderboard reports
\begin{equation}
  \bar{s}_{\mathrm{in}} = \langle s_c \rangle_{c \in \mathcal{C}(\mathcal{M})},
  \qquad
  \bar{s}_{\mathrm{out}} = \langle s_c \rangle_{c \notin \mathcal{C}(\mathcal{M})},
  \qquad
  \pi = \bar{s}_{\mathrm{out}} / \bar{s}_{\mathrm{in}},
  \label{eq:split}
\end{equation}
with $\pi$ the transfer penalty. A run that diverges or fails to converge
scores $s_c = \infty$ and is excluded from the means.

\subsection{A-priori regression as a diagnostic}

Candidate closure terms are also evaluated directly from the DNS and their
coefficients solved for by least squares, with the target normalized by $k$
so that it is the structure parameter. The library admits only quantities a
RANS solver possesses; the correlation of every column with the target is
reported; and fits are evaluated on data not used to produce them (the
opposite half of the plate, and the independent DNS).

\subsection{Reproducibility}

Every number, table and figure in this paper is produced by a pipeline
stage from version-controlled inputs, and the values in the text are
generated from the results files. The project's questions, answers and
evidence are recorded in its metadata, and a check reports any answer whose
cited evidence has changed since it was last reviewed. The benchmark is
meant to be forked: a derived project registers a closure or case, re-runs
the pipeline, regenerates this document, and records the project it was
derived from.

\subsection{What transfers}\label{sec:transfers}

The structure parameter $a_1 = -\langle u'v'\rangle / 2k$, integrated across
the layer, is $\result[aOneTransitional]$ at the downstream end of the plate
and $\result[aOneJimenez] \pm \result[aOneJimenezSd]$ across
$Re_\theta = 4000$--$6500$ in the ZPG DNS: agreement to
\result[aOneRelDiff]\,\% across a fourfold change in $Re_\theta$
\citep[Bradshaw's constant,][]{Bradshaw1967}.

The dissipation coefficient $C_\epsilon = \epsilon L / u'^3$, with
$L = \delta_{99}$ and $u' = \sqrt{2\bar{k}/3}$, obtained from the exact
integral balance
\begin{equation}
  \epsilon_{\rm int} = P_{\rm int} - \frac{{\rm d}}{{\rm d}x}
  \int U k \, {\rm d}y ,
  \label{eq:intbudget}
\end{equation}
is $\result[CepsTurb] \pm \result[CepsTurbSd]$ over $Re_\theta = 900$--$1500$
on the plate and $\result[CepsJimenez]$ in the ZPG DNS. Through transition
it varies by a factor of \result[CepsRatio], rising from $\result[CepsPre]$;
$P/\epsilon$ peaks at $\result[PoverEpsPeak]$ near
$x \approx \result[PoverEpsPeakX]$, coinciding with the peak in turbulent
energy storage ($x \approx 246$) and the entropy minimum ($x \approx 233$)
\citep[cf.][]{Vassilicos2015, LiuFangFang2021}.

\subsection{What does not transfer}\label{sec:fails}

Regressing $-\langle u'v'\rangle/k$ on \result[NTerms] dimensionless groups
formed from $\partial U/\partial y$, $\partial^2 U/\partial y^2$, $y$, $\nu$
and $k$ gives $R^2 = \result[StressInSample]$ in sample; coefficients fitted
downstream of $x = 450$ and applied upstream give
$R^2 = \result[StressDownToUp]$, and coefficients fitted on the plate and
applied to the ZPG DNS give $R^2 = \result[StressToJimenez]$, although the
latter fits itself to $R^2 = \result[JimenezSelfFit]$. The normalized
disagreement between upstream and downstream coefficient vectors is
$\result[StressCoeffDisagree]$, its maximum. The unclosed residual of the
$k$ budget gives $R^2 = \result[EpsInSample]$ in sample, $\result[EpsUpToDown]$
and $\result[EpsDownToUp]$ out (Table~\ref{tab:transfer},

\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conc}

% TODO (human): rewrite.

Under a rule that never averages in-sample with out-of-sample cases, the
clipping closure is the best model on its calibration case and the
worst-transferring one in the fast tier; Launder--Sharma is the best fast-
tier model out of sample and unstable on the separated hills in OpenFOAM;
the published transition models lead the OpenFOAM tier; and the two tiers
rank the closures they share differently. In-sample agreement, correlation
coefficients and structure rankings did not distinguish a transferable
relation from a case-specific fit; out-of-sample transfer, a
trivial-coordinate baseline and repetition of the inner fit under different
seeds did. The suite, the scoring rule and every number here are
regenerated by the project's pipeline, and the project is built to be
forked.
